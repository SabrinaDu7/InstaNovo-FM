## Rules (owner, 15/10/2026)

1. The baseline is the batch-2,048 configuration (the one that reproduces the released 40M; the paper's stated
   batch 1,024 does not).
2. **The training objective is not changed**: masking, the 10 Da hint, the heads, the losses and the checkpoint
   criterion stay as shipped. An architectural change that cannot run without touching the objective is allowed only
   if that dependency is stated in so many words.
3. Every result is at 40M on MCFM.
4. Dropout is a generalisation ingredient, not an architectural choice: it stays at 0.1 in every arm.

## Baseline

`configs/foundational.yaml` with `dataset=mcfm`, 1,024 spectra per forward pass, `grad_accumulation=2`, 90,000
steps, one H100, `torch.compile` on. Run `train-40M-mcfm-90k-b2048` (job 23151784, 18 h 57 min), seed 101,
checkpoints every 10,000 steps; its validation metrics at every 10,000 steps are in
`docs/references/rerun/train_40M_mcfm_90k_b2048/metrics.json`. Learning rate: linear warmup over 4,500 steps, hold at
1e-4 until step 31,500, cosine to zero at 90,000 (`warmup_frac 0.05`, `lr_hold_frac 0.3`).

Overrides of every run besides the arm's own:

```
training_steps=90000 grad_accumulation=2 num_workers=8 +mp_sharing_strategy=file_system keep_model_every_interval=True post_training_evaluation.enabled=False
```

## Arms

Each arm is the baseline with one change. "Why" names the observation in section 2 of the running notes that
motivates it. Parameter counts are measured by building each arm on the workstation (15/10).

| Arm | Change | Why | Override on the baseline | Parameters |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| `seed102` | nothing: the baseline again | E3: with one seed, no one-run difference can be read; every other arm is judged against the spread between seeds 101 and 102 | `seed=102` | 39.87 M |
| `meta-precursor` | metadata tokens carrying precursor mass, charge bin, fragmentation type and collision energy; the instrument is left out so no search table is needed and the change is precursor information alone | B2: no precursor information reaches the encoder, the cheapest strong feature for identity | `model.meta_token.enabled=true model.meta_token.include_instrument=false` | 40.03 M |
| `pa-mask2mask` | pairwise attention bias (Fourier features of the mass difference between every two peaks) kept between masked tokens; pairs between a masked and a visible token are zeroed, as every pair touching a mask is in the released PA checkpoints | C1: the bias is switched off exactly where reconstruction happens, a candidate reason the paper's TS·PA variant lost | `model.architecture.relative_bias.type=pa model.architecture.relative_bias.config.pa_mask_to_mask=true` | 39.90 M |
| `ionladder` | residue-mass soft matches to 40 neighbours each side, added to every peak token through a zero-initialised gate; masked peaks are excluded from the windows | a chemistry-aware input the authors built and never reported (the ladder relationship the attention heads are said to discover on their own) | `model.ion_ladder.enabled=true` | 39.92 M |
| `enc-fourier` | peak encoder: DreaMS-style Fourier bank, periods 2.5 to 3,750 Da, trainable | B1: the shipped bank spans one decade of periods and did not learn | `model.peak_encoder.type=fourier` | 38.69 M |
| `enc-dual` | peak encoder: the multiscale bank plus 1,024 RBFs through a zero-initialised gate | B1 | `model.peak_encoder.type=dual` | 41.25 M |
| `ffn3072` | feed-forward width 3,072 (the 89M's) | C2: the 40M's ratio 1.33 is an untested cost cut; a width test, not parameter-matched | `model.dim_feedforward=3072` | 68.20 M |
| `peaks300` | 300 peaks kept instead of 200 | B4: 37.8 % of MCFM test spectra hit the 200 cap; attention cost 2.25 times | `model.n_peaks=300` (micro-batch `train_batch_size=512 grad_accumulation=4 predict_batch_size=512`, same global batch) | 39.87 M |

Dropped: `dropout0` (rule 4). Parked under rule 2: every change to the loss, the targets, the masking or the hint
(A1 to A6 and B3 in the notes), and a trained pooling head (D1). Phase 2 arms that need code (`prenorm-gelu`,
`no-latent`, `swiglu`) are listed in the notes and not part of the current goal.

**Caveat on two arms.** `meta-precursor` and `pa-mask2mask` leave the objective untouched but make its task easier:
with the precursor mass known, a masked peak's complementary ion fixes its m/z; with exact mass differences among
masked peaks, their independent 10 Da hints can be pooled. Their masked-m/z validation metrics are therefore not an
equal-terms comparison with the baseline's, and their mass, m/z and charge probes are trivial by construction for
`meta-precursor`. The downstream protocols (retrieval, the other probes, peak-level and geometry) are the test.

## How a change is judged

The suite's paper protocols on the corpus splits, run by `scripts/reproduce/result_arm.py` exactly as for the
released checkpoints: `paper-probes-retrieval`, `paper-peak-level` and `paper-geometry` on LCFM test,
`paper-probes-retrieval` on MCFM test, the trainer's own validation on MCFM validation (row 23 of the 40M table),
plus the fragment-group diagnostic of `scripts/analysis/fragment_reconstruction.py` when wanted. Every arm is read at
step 90,001. A change counts when it clears the spread between the two baseline seeds on retrieval and fragment
accuracy without losing a probe. The table is `results.md`.

## Profile of the pairwise-attention path (16/10/2026, workstation RTX 4060)

Why the PA arm costs 3.03 s a step against 0.74 on the H100. Measured by `scripts/analysis/profile_pa.py` (one
optimizer step as the trainer runs it, micro-batch 32, 10 steps; `profile_pa.md`, `profile_pa_chunked.md`) and
`scripts/analysis/sdpa_backend_check.py` (`sdpa_backend_check.log`).

| Variant, batch 32 | Step | Forward | Backward | Peak memory | Attention range | Pairwise features | Per-layer projection |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| baseline | 0.178 s | 0.040 | 0.123 | 1.06 GiB | 0.029 s | | |
| baseline compiled (as trained) | 0.151 s | 0.032 | 0.104 | 1.32 GiB | | | |
| pa | 0.429 s | 0.134 | 0.280 | 2.59 GiB | 0.130 s | 0.023 s | 0.014 s |
| pa-mask2mask | 0.431 s | 0.134 | 0.281 | 2.68 GiB | 0.130 s | 0.023 s | 0.014 s |
| pa, 16 chunks (the trainer's chunking) | 0.475 s | | | 1.74 GiB | 0.128 s | 0.023 s | 0.017 s |
| pa compiled | fails: Triton compilation error in Inductor's kernel (`Cannot make_shape_compatible: incompatible dimensions at index 2: 64 and 2`) | | | | | | |

**Cause, confirmed.** The per-layer bias leaves `BatchedPairwiseProjection` as a `view(C, L, L, N, H).permute(3, 0, 4, 1, 2)`
and reaches SDPA as a (B, 12, 201, 201) fp16 tensor with strides (484812, 1, 2412, 12): heads innermost, last
dimension stride 12. PyTorch's dispatcher refuses every fused kernel ("All fused kernels require the last dimension of
the input to have stride 1 ... Attn_mask.stride(-1): 12") and attention runs `_scaled_dot_product_attention_math`,
which materialises the fp32 score matrices of all nine layers, forward and again in the checkpointed backward
(`profile_pa.md`: `aten::_scaled_dot_product_attention_math` 0.099 s, `aten::copy_` 0.081 s, `aten::to` 0.056 s per
step). The baseline's (B, 1, 1, L) padding mask is accepted by the memory-efficient kernel (`fmha_cutlass`). Attention
is therefore 4.5 times the baseline's per step; the Fourier features and the MLP over every peak pair (0.023 s) and
the projection (0.014 s) are a third of that; chunking the two PA modules 16 ways adds 10 % and saves a third of the
memory; `torch.compile` cannot be applied to the path and the baseline loses its 15 % compile gain too.

**Fix, applied 16/10 (owner's go):** `BatchedPairwiseProjection._forward_chunk` materialises the permuted
(N, C, H, L, L) tensor once (`.contiguous()`), so every per-layer bias reaches SDPA with strides (484812, 40401, 201, 1)
and the memory-efficient kernel runs (`sdpa_backend_check.log`: `EFFICIENT_ATTENTION: ran`; flash refuses any bias by
design). Same maths: on one batch in eval mode with the hint noise reseeded, the math implementation and the fused
kernel differ by 7.3e-4 at most and 8.3e-5 on average on group logits of scale 0.16 (fp16 kernel precision; the same
kernel twice gives 0). Re-profiled (`profile_pa_contiguous.md`, same settings):

| Variant, batch 32 | Step before | Step after | Attention range before | after | Projection range before | after |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| baseline | 0.178 s | 0.178 s | 0.029 s | 0.029 s | | |
| pa | 0.429 s | 0.326 s | 0.130 s | 0.043 s | 0.014 s | 0.020 s |
| pa-mask2mask | 0.431 s | 0.326 s | 0.130 s | 0.042 s | 0.014 s | 0.021 s |

The PA step went from 2.42 to 1.83 times the baseline here; the projection's extra 0.006 s is the copy. What remains
of the PA overhead is the pairwise features (0.023 s), the projection (0.020 s), the bias-carrying attention (0.014 s
over flash) and their recomputation in the checkpointed backward, plus the lost `torch.compile`. The H100 ratio after
the fix is measured by the local speed runs below rather than extrapolated.

**Local speed runs (16/10, RTX 4060, after the fix):** baseline and `pa-mask2mask` trained for 6,000 steps each on the
two local MCFM training shards with the real training loop (loader workers, checkpointing, validation every 1,000
steps on the 40,000-row file, `torch.compile` where the trainer allows it), one after the other, by
`~/Documents/experiments/Arc/instanovo-fm-runs/local-speed/run.sh`; exports under
`docs/references/rerun/train_local-speed-*/`. PA at micro-batch 64 does not fit the 8 GB card (out of memory at the
first step), so the pair ran at micro-batch 32; the batch-64 baseline is kept as an extra point.

| Run | Micro-batch | Seconds per optimizer step (elapsed clock over 6,000 steps) | Peak GPU memory | Validation at step 6,001: bin accuracy, median ppm, loss |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| baseline | 64 | 0.311 | 3.9 GB | 5.27 %, 8,240, 2.167 |
| baseline | 32 | 0.164 | 2.7 GB | 5.09 %, 8,658, 2.192 |
| pa-mask2mask | 32 | 0.337 | 4.9 GB | 46.98 %, 422, 0.834 |

**PA costs 2.05 times the baseline per step in the training loop after the fix** (4.1 times before it on the H100).
Projected for the H100 at the baseline's 0.745 s: about 1.53 s per step, 38 h for 90,000 steps plus start-up, which
is still over the 36-hour limit of the queued job 24017761 by a few hours (inferred from the 4060 ratio; the H100's
kernels may scale differently). Options for the owner: resubmit with a 48-hour limit (same 3-day partition), or drop
the arm.

**The PA arm's validation metrics are not comparable, measured.** At equal steps its masked-m/z reconstruction is in a
different regime: bin accuracy 47 % against 5 %, median error 422 ppm against 8,658, loss 0.83 against 2.19. The
group accuracy (10 Da) rose from 46 % to 91 %, which is what pooling the 10 Da hints through exact masked-to-masked
differences predicts; the offset accuracy (0.2 Da within the group) rose from 12 % to 48 %, more than pooled 10 Da
hints can give, so the exact pairwise differences carry more absolute position than the option's "safe, only Δm/z"
note assumes (inferred; a dedicated leakage check would settle it). Whether the *embedding* gains anything is only
answerable with the suite's protocols on a PA checkpoint; the two local step-6,001 checkpoints exist for that.

## Per-arm procedure when a full run ends

1. `cp $RUNS/arch-<arm>/checkpoints/model_epoch_*_step_90001.ckpt $CHECKPOINTS/instanovo-fm-mcfm-90k-arch-<arm>-step90k.ckpt`
2. `source .envrc && ~/scratch/venvs/instanovo-fm/bin/python scripts/train/export_metrics.py arch-<arm> --job <job id>`
3. `sbatch --time=02:59:00 --job-name=r<N>_arch-<arm> scripts/reproduce/submit.sh scripts/reproduce/result_arm.py --model arch-<arm> --result <N>` for N in 1, 2, 3, 5
4. `python scripts/reproduce/arch_results.py`; commit the new files under `docs/references/rerun/` and `results.md`.

For a smoke run: step 2 with `smoke10k-<arm>`, then `python scripts/train/smoke_results.py`.
