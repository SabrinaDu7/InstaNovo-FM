Traced in the installed InstaNovo greedy decoder (`instanovo/inference/greedy_search.py`, the mass-constrained step): residues that do not fit the remaining mass are masked to -inf and the next token is the arg-max of the masked distribution, but the score adds the model's unmasked log-probability of the chosen token; when no residue fits, the padding token is the only unmasked one, the sequence ends there, and its unmasked log-probability (hundreds of nats) lands in the score while the token list written to the CSV stops before it.
